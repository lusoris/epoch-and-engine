# Research Dossier: The Quantum Revolution

## Overview
This dossier examines the emergence and development of quantum mechanics as a revolutionary scientific theory that fundamentally changed our understanding of atomic and subatomic phenomena.

## Primary Sources

### Key Historical Papers
1. Planck's 1900 black-body radiation paper
   - Original publication in Annalen der Physik

2. Einstein's 1905 photoelectric effect paper (Nobel Prize winning work)
   - Original publications in Annalen der Physik

3. Schrödinger's wave equation (1926)
   - Original paper "Quantisierung als Eigenwertproblem"

4. Heisenberg's uncertainty principle (1927)
   - Original paper "Über den anschaulichen Inhalt der quantentheoretischen Kinematik und Mechanik"

- Wikipedia page on the history of quantum mechanics

## Research Methodology
This research will utilize original scientific papers, contemporary academic correspondence, and documented scientific debates to trace the development of quantum theory from its inception to modern applications.
